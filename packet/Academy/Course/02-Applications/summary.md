# Applications and hands-on practice

Session 2 practice guide for Justin’s block on November 20.

## Pick up where you left off

Open your Academy folder in a new chat and say:

> Remind me what I was exploring.

Check that your companion names the saved file it read. It then asks two short
questions, one at a time: what you tried or noticed since Session 1, and what
you would like it to help you produce. A sentence or two for each is enough.

## Build a dashboard

The practice uses a fictional HVAC service company with six branches in
Louisiana and twelve weeks of reports. Leadership meets on Monday and wants to
see what changed and which branches need attention. Both files are in this
folder and on the follow-along page, links.obney.ai/academy-c2-build. All
figures are invented.

- `branch-weekly-service.csv`: service calls, same-day completion, response
  time, callbacks, parts backorders, revenue and complaints, by branch and week.
- `branch-weekly-staffing.csv`: technicians, available hours and overtime for
  the same branches and weeks.

The exercise repeats one loop: describe what you want, inspect the result, ask
for changes.

1. **Start with one report.** Ask your companion to explain the service file
   before it builds anything. Then ask for a first dashboard, open it, and ask
   for one change in your own words.
2. **Add the staffing report.** Ask how the two files connect by branch and
   week, and what doesn’t line up, before anything changes. Staffing can raise
   a question about service. It does not prove a cause.
3. **Check what the dashboard claims.** Trace a total, a trend and an outlier
   back to the rows. Check whether combining the files dropped branches,
   duplicated rows or turned missing values into zeros. Keep confirmed errors
   separate from open questions.
4. **Ask where next Monday’s data would come from.** A useful dashboard needs
   fresh reports. Talk through where they come from today and who would review
   an update. This is a discussion; nothing is connected in class.

If you can’t see the dashboard, ask your assistant to save it as a single HTML
file, then open that file in your browser.

## What to save

Keep the original files in `Course/` unchanged. Save your work beside
`Course/`, in a `Builds/` folder:

- the dashboard;
- a corrected copy of the data, if you fixed anything;
- a short note on what you built, what you checked and what is still uncertain.

Open the saved dashboard yourself to confirm it is there.

Near the end of the block you’ll be asked where something like this could help
in your work. If an idea or question comes up, ask your companion to save a
short note of it with your other saved work. It is something to explore in
Sessions 3–5, not a commitment, and it stays separate from the class dashboard.
