## Slide 8

GCLTC HIRING  ·  THE STARTING POINT
A Familiar Problem: Hiring Across Many Sites
WHAT WAS GETTING IN THE WAY
THE SITUATION
Status lived in inboxes
Many facilities, one recruiting team
Openings come in by Job Order ticket or on a Friday administrator call
Applicants come through Indeed; facilities interview and run background checks
Offers need administrator and regional approval before the new hire packet goes out
Leadership — regional management, the COO, and owners — needs regular status
No single view of where each candidate stood.
Rules lived in people’s heads
Eligibility and paperwork rules applied inconsistently.
Handoffs were unclear
Recruiter, facility, and hiring manager each assumed someone else had it.
Reporting took hours
Updates were rebuilt by hand every time.

You are an operations analyst helping me document our hiring process for our GCLTC facilities.
I’ve attached my notes, a sample hire-confirmation email, and our open-position tracker.
Map the current process from “position opens” to “new hire packet complete.” For each step, list who owns it, what triggers it, what document or system is used, and who it’s handed to next.

THE PROMPT (CONDENSED)  ·  RUNS AS A SCHEDULED TASK
Every weekday at 7:00 a.m., search my mailbox for emails from GCLTC facilities in the last 24 hours. Keep only applications, offers and recommended hires. Group by candidate — position, shift, facility, latest status, rate or bonus.
