# Try these with your assistant

Practice questions for Justin’s block. They match the deck and the follow-along
page.

## Pick up where you left off

1. Remind me what I was exploring. Then ask me two short questions, one at a
   time: what I tried or noticed since our first session, and what I wish you
   could help me produce.

## Build and check the dashboard

2. Read `branch-weekly-service.csv` and explain what it contains: the columns,
   the date range and anything unclear. Don’t build anything yet.
3. Build a dashboard for our Monday leadership meeting that helps us see what
   changed and which branches need attention.
4. Here’s a staffing report for the same branches and weeks,
   `branch-weekly-staffing.csv`. Explain how it connects to the service report
   and anything that doesn’t line up. Then add staffing capacity to the dashboard.
5. What in this dashboard might be wrong or unsupported? Show the source rows
   behind each finding, check whether combining the files dropped, duplicated or
   zero-filled any rows, and separate confirmed errors from open questions.
6. Save this dashboard in a Builds folder in my Academy folder without changing
   the original files. Then save a short note about what we built, what we
   checked and what’s still uncertain.

After each version, open the result and ask for one change in your own words.

## After the build

8. Does this dashboard call an AI model when it runs, or does it calculate by

9. Help me think about where something like this could help in my work. Ask me
   one question at a time, then save a short note of the idea.
